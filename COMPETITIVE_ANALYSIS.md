# Northamptonshire Web Design - Competitive Analysis
## Prepared for WebOrb (web-orb.uk) | March 2026

---

## Executive Summary

This analysis covers **12 confirmed local competitors** operating in the Northamptonshire web design market (Northampton, Kettering, Corby, Wellingborough, and surrounding towns). The market ranges from budget freelancers charging ~£200 for basic sites to full-service agencies quoting £5,000+. A small number of competitors use **monthly/subscription models**, but the majority operate on traditional one-off project pricing. Very few local competitors specifically target tradespeople -- this is primarily served by national trade-specific providers.

## Key Market Insights

### 1. Subscription/Monthly Models Are Rare Locally
Only **3 out of 12** local competitors offer any form of monthly/subscription model:
- **it'seeze** (franchise subscription: £28-59/month + upfront fee, minimum 12-24 months)
- **MPS Creative** (£10/month rental, no contract -- but very basic quality)
- **Arwen Web Design** (monthly plan, 2-year contract, price undisclosed)

One more (Merlin's Web) offers a payment plan that spreads the cost but isn't truly recurring. This represents a clear **gap in the market** for a quality subscription web design service locally.

### 2. Pricing Clusters
The local market falls into three distinct pricing bands:
- **Budget (£100-£600):** MPS Creative, WebsitesCo, Northants Website Design (now retired). Basic sites, often template-based.
- **Mid-range (£500-£2,200):** TJ Design, it'seeze, Merlin's Web, Poppy Design Studio, Arwen. Professional sites with clear packages.
- **Premium (£2,000+):** Rak Design, Newedge, Spartan, WTBI, Seventy9. Custom work, no public pricing, quotes only.

### 3. Almost Nobody Targets Tradespeople Locally
**None of the 12 local competitors explicitly target tradespeople or trades businesses.** This niche is currently served by national providers such as:
- **Trade Pixels** (£1,499 one-off or 12 x £150/month)
- **HungryWolf** (pay monthly websites for trades UK-wide)
- **Websites 4 Trades** (£30/month, unlimited changes)
- **BUILT for Trades** (UK-wide trades specialist)
- **Stay On The Tools** (trades-specific with local SEO focus)

This represents a **significant opportunity** for a local provider to own the "web design for tradespeople in Northamptonshire" positioning.

### 4. Common Service Bundles
Most competitors offer a similar core:
- Web design (WordPress dominant)
- SEO (basic on-page)
- Hosting
- Domain registration
- SSL certificates

Differentiators include: branding/logo design, photography/video, Google Ads management, app development, and print design.

### 5. Technology Stack
- **WordPress** dominates -- used by DeType, CSM, Poppy, TJ Design, Arwen, Daniel Barker, and others
- **Proprietary CMS:** it'seeze (franchise platform), Seventy9 (custom built CMS)
- **Silverstripe:** Newedge
- **Custom coded:** WebOrb (hand-coded/Webflow/Shopify)

WebOrb's "no page builders, no shortcuts" custom-coded approach is genuinely unusual in this market and a strong differentiator.

### 6. Delivery Speed
Most local competitors quote **3-10 weeks** for website delivery. WebOrb's stated **2-week delivery** is significantly faster than any local competitor and is a major competitive advantage.

## Opportunities for WebOrb

1. **Subscription model gap:** Only it'seeze offers a true ongoing subscription locally, and they lock clients into a proprietary platform. A high-quality subscription model with portable websites would be differentiated.

2. **Trades niche is wide open locally:** No local competitor owns this space. National trades-specific providers exist but lack local knowledge and relationships.

3. **Speed advantage:** 2-week delivery vs 3-10 weeks industry standard is a compelling USP.

4. **Quality advantage at mid-market pricing:** Most affordable local options (£100-£600) deliver basic template sites. WebOrb's custom-coded approach at mid-range pricing would stand out.

5. **Google Business Profile / Local SEO specialism:** Few local competitors offer dedicated GBP optimisation as a standalone service.

6. **Monthly model pricing sweet spot:** Between MPS Creative's £10/month (too cheap/basic) and it'seeze's £28-59/month (franchise lock-in), there's room for a quality monthly offering in the £30-£80/month range.
