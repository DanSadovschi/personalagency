Monthly Fans is a platform built around a simple idea: help artists grow their Spotify presence through a community of engaged monthly supporters. The project was one of the most technically demanding in our portfolio — a full e-commerce platform with custom animations, user accounts, and a flexible subscription model.

## What we built

We built the entire platform from the ground up. Users can subscribe to support their favourite artists on a monthly basis or make a one-time purchase. Each user gets a personal account where they can manage their subscriptions, upgrade their plan, or cancel at any time.

The interface was built with complex, custom JavaScript animations throughout — the kind of motion that makes a product feel premium and alive. SVG graphics were used throughout to keep the visual quality high across all screen sizes without compromising performance.

## The results

Monthly Fans launched as a fully functional, production-ready platform. The subscription and account management system works seamlessly, and the animations give the product a distinctive identity that stands out in the music tech space.
