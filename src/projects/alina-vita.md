Alina Vita is a massage therapist based in Corby with a background in pharmacy — a rare combination that shapes how she approaches every session. She needed a website that felt as intentional as her practice: calm, clinical, and unmistakably premium.

## What we built

We designed and built a single-page website that guides visitors from introduction to booking without friction. The hero section leads with a strong headline — "The Power of Intentional Touch" — paired with professional photography that sets the tone immediately.

The treatments section presents all 11 services in a clean, numbered list grouped by category: full body massage, targeted body treatments, and specialised sessions. Each treatment shows its starting price and expands to reveal a full description — giving visitors the information they need without overwhelming the page.

The about section tells Alina's story — her pharmaceutical background, her approach to massage, and her qualifications — in a way that builds trust before the visitor ever picks up the phone. A WhatsApp button and booking CTA are always within reach.

The colour palette — soft teal accents against warm neutrals — was chosen to feel calming without being generic. Typography mixes editorial serif headings with clean sans-serif body text, giving the site a distinctive character that stands apart from typical wellness templates.

## The results

The site launched and started generating booking enquiries within the first week. Visitors arriving from Google and social media are converting into sessions — the site does the trust-building work before the first message is even sent.
