Legacy Ventures Performance is a leadership consultancy built by a former military professional. Their clients are senior executives and high-performance teams — people who make fast decisions and have no patience for ambiguity. The brief was clear: build something that communicates precision, credibility, and premium positioning from the first second.

## What we built

We built everything from zero — visual identity, custom graphics, page architecture, and the full production build. The design is disciplined and restrained: strong typographic hierarchy, deliberately generous white space, and a colour palette that reads as authoritative without tipping into corporate grey.

All graphics and visual assets were created in-house — no stock imagery, no generic icons. Every element on the page was built to reinforce the same message: this firm operates at an elite level.

The copy was written for senior decision-makers. Services are presented with clarity — what the engagement is, who it's for, what happens next. The contact flow goes straight to a scheduled call, bypassing the generic enquiry form entirely.

## The results

LVP launched with a site that changed how prospects perceived them before any conversation took place. Within 60 days, qualified inbound enquiries from executives were arriving through the booking flow. The client reported that prospects were arriving at calls pre-sold on the approach — the site was doing the qualification work before they picked up the phone.
