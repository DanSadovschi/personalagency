Blitz Pikado Bar is a venue with a clear identity: stylish drinks and serious darts. They needed a website that matched the energy of the place — something that would get people excited to visit, sign up for tournaments, and book a table.

## What we built

We created a retro comic book-inspired UI that sets the tone immediately. Bold colours, dynamic layouts, and a visual style that feels as much like a poster as a website. The design captures the competitive, social spirit of the venue without sacrificing usability.

Beyond aesthetics, the site does real work: a tournament section with event listings, an online booking system for table reservations, and a fully responsive experience across all devices. Navigation is seamless — whether someone is checking the tournament schedule or booking a spot for the weekend.

## The results

Since launching, Blitz Bar has seen a measurable uplift in online event sign-ups and table bookings. The website has strengthened the bar's presence in the local competitive darts scene and become a central hub for their community.
