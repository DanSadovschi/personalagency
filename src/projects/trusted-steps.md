Trusted Steps is a pet care service run by Pete and Laura in Corby, Northamptonshire. They offer dog walking, dog home visits, cat visits, and small animal care — all fully insured, Pet First Aid trained, and DBS checked. They needed a website that made pet owners feel confident handing over their keys.

## What we built

We built a multi-page website that covers everything a pet owner needs to know before making contact: who Pete and Laura are, what services they offer, how much it costs, and a gallery of happy animals in their care.

The homepage leads with a full-width hero image of a dog on a Corby park path — immediately local and real. Trust badges (Fully Insured, Pet First Aid, DBS Checked) sit below the fold, followed by a personal introduction with photos of both Pete and Laura.

The services section breaks down four distinct offerings — dog walking, dog home visits, cat visits, and small animal care — each with its own image, description, and clear pricing. A dedicated pricing page gives visitors full transparency before they even make contact.

The photo gallery showcases real animals in their care, not stock photography. The contact page includes a form, phone number, WhatsApp link, and their coverage area — making it easy for local pet owners to reach out however they prefer.

The design uses warm earth tones — deep greens and soft creams — that feel approachable and trustworthy without being childish. The overall tone is professional but personal, which matches Pete and Laura's approach exactly.

## The results

Within weeks of launching, Trusted Steps was fully booked. The website gave them immediate credibility in a market where most competitors rely on Facebook pages and word of mouth alone. Pet owners now find them through Google and arrive at the enquiry form already convinced.
