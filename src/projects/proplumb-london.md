ProPlumb London were relying entirely on word of mouth and a few old leads from a directory listing. They had no website and no Google presence — invisible to anyone who hadn't already heard of them.

## What we built

We built a fast, clean website focused on emergency and booked plumbing work across North London. The copy was written to convert high-intent visitors — people searching for a plumber right now — with a prominent click-to-call button above the fold and a simple booking form.

Each service got its own dedicated page: boiler repairs, bathroom fitting, leak detection, drain unblocking. This gave Google more to rank and gave visitors a clearer path to the information they were looking for.

We also set up and optimised their Google Business Profile, targeting the boroughs where they work most.

## The results

Within six weeks of the site going live, ProPlumb were ranking on page one for key search terms across their target area. Calls from Google increased week on week from week three onward. They've since expanded their coverage area off the back of the new demand coming in.
