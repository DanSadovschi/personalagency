ALT DIGITAL is a digital marketing agency. A marketing agency's website is its most visible piece of work — it either demonstrates the standard they hold themselves to, or it doesn't. The previous site didn't. The redesign had to fix that and give them a platform that could convert its own traffic.

## What we built

We built everything from zero: brand identity, all custom graphics and visual assets, UI design, and the complete production build. Nothing was borrowed from templates or stock libraries — the entire visual system was created to position ALT DIGITAL as a credible, premium choice in a crowded market.

The copy was written to speak directly to business owners who are evaluating agencies: specific about what's delivered, honest about how it works, and focused on outcomes rather than process. The structure follows the natural decision path — who this is for, what they get, why it works, and how to start.

All page graphics and section illustrations were designed in-house to reinforce the agency's identity throughout rather than breaking up the design with generic imagery.

## The results

Inbound enquiries grew in the months after launch. More importantly, the quality of enquiries improved — prospects arriving through the site were better qualified and more aligned with the type of work ALT DIGITAL wanted to take on. The site became the agency's strongest new business tool.
