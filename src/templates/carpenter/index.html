Our Specialisms

What We Make

Every piece is designed around your space, your taste and your life — then built to order in our South London workshop.

Fitted Wardrobes

Floor-to-ceiling fitted wardrobes designed to make the most of every inch. Internal layouts tailored to your storage needs — hanging rails, drawers, shelving and more.

Bespoke Furniture

One-off dining tables, desks, beds and statement pieces crafted from solid timber. Designed with you, finished to your exact specifications and built to be passed down.

Kitchen Units & Islands

Handmade kitchen cabinets in shaker, in-frame or contemporary styles. Painted or veneered, soft-close throughout and built with the same joinery standards as fine furniture.

Alcove & Shelving

Alcove cupboards, floating shelves and bespoke bookcase runs that transform awkward recesses into beautiful, functional storage — a London home essential.

Home Office Fit-Out

Built-in desks, shelving walls, file drawer banks and cable management — the complete home office designed around how you actually work.

Bedroom Suites

Complete bespoke bedroom furniture — fitted wardrobes, bedside tables, dressing tables and storage beds — all matched to create a cohesive, considered room.

How It Works

From First Chat
to Finished Piece

A straightforward process designed to make getting bespoke joinery simple, certain and stress-free.

1
Site Visit & Measure

We visit your home to measure up, talk through your ideas and understand how the space will be used. No charge, no obligation.

2
Design & Quote

Within a week you receive scaled drawings, a material sample board and a fixed-price quote — no hidden extras.

3
Built in Our Workshop

Your piece is crafted by hand in our South London workshop using traditional joinery techniques and premium materials.

4
Delivered & Installed

Our own team installs everything to a perfect fit. We leave the room spotless and sign off with a 5-year workmanship guarantee.

What We Use

Materials We Work With

We source responsibly and work with materials we genuinely love. Here are the four we reach for most — each with its own character.

Natural Oak

Warm honey tones with a tight, pronounced grain. Incredibly durable, ages beautifully and takes both oil and paint finishes superbly. Our most popular choice.

Dark Walnut

Rich chocolate-brown with a fine, even grain that gives a luxurious, contemporary feel. Exceptional for statement furniture and high-end fitted pieces.

Painted MDF

Precision-machined MDF with a hard lacquer or eggshell finish. Clean, crisp lines in any colour — perfect for shaker kitchens and contemporary fitted furniture.

Reclaimed Wood

Salvaged timbers with a lifetime of character — knots, nail holes and colour variation that no new wood can replicate. Every piece is genuinely one of a kind.
