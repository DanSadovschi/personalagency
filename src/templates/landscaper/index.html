What We Do

Garden services built to last

From a weekly lawn cut to a complete garden transformation — GreenCraft delivers quality workmanship at every scale.

Lawn Care

Professional mowing, edging, scarification, aeration and lawn feeding programmes tailored to your grass type and growing season.

Garden Design

Bespoke planting plans, full garden redesigns and 3D visual concepts — creating outdoor spaces that reflect your lifestyle and vision.

Hedge & Tree Work

Precision hedge trimming, crown lifting, tree surgery and safe removal. All work carried out by qualified, insured arborists.

Patio & Decking

Natural stone and porcelain patio laying, repointing and restoration. Composite or timber decking design, build and annual pressure washing.

Planting & Borders

Seasonal colour, structural planting, cottage-style borders and spring bulb schemes that deliver year-round interest and texture.

Fencing & Gates

Supply and installation of close-board, post-and-rail and decorative fencing. Bespoke gates, repairs and annual staining treatments.

Year-Round Expertise

Work that follows the seasons

Every season brings different needs. Our seasonal programmes keep your garden thriving all twelve months of the year.

🌾

Lawn Scarification & Aeration

Remove thatch, relieve compaction and kick-start healthy growth for the season ahead.

🌸

Spring Planting

Hardy annuals, perennials and seasonal bedding planted out once frost risk has passed.

🧹

Border Clearance

Cut back overwintered stems, weed thoroughly and prepare beds for a fresh growing year.

💦

Pressure Washing

Deep-clean patios, paths and driveways to remove winter algae and slippery moss growth.

🪵

Mulching

Apply bark or compost mulch to borders to retain moisture and suppress spring weeds.

✂️

Regular Mowing & Edging

Fortnightly or weekly cuts with crisp lawn edges to keep your garden looking immaculate.

🌳

Hedge Trimming

Shape formal and informal hedges at the peak of their growing season for defined lines.

💧

Irrigation Setup

Design and install drip or pop-up irrigation systems to beat the summer water restrictions.

🌿

Weeding & Feeding

Hand-weeding borders, lawn fertiliser programmes and foliar feeds to sustain lush growth.

🌺

Annual Planting

Fill containers and bedding schemes with vibrant summer annuals for season-long colour.

🍁

Leaf Clearance

Regular collection and composting of fallen leaves to protect lawns and paths from rot.

🌱

Lawn Overseeding

Overseed thin or bare patches in autumn soil to restore a thick, even sward before winter.

🌷

Bulb Planting

Plant spring-flowering tulip, daffodil and allium bulbs at depth for a spectacular display.

🪓

Tree Surgery

Crown reduction, deadwood removal and formative pruning while trees enter dormancy.

🏡

Winter Prep

Protect tender plants, drain irrigation, lag pipes and store garden furniture safely away.

📐

Garden Planning & Design

The ideal time to plan a redesign — we survey, design and present concepts ready for spring.

🪨

Hard Landscaping

Lay new patios, paths, steps and raised beds during the quieter months with minimal disruption.

🪜

Fence Installation

Post-setting is ideal in winter — firmer ground and no plants to work around.

🐸

Pond Maintenance

Net removal, pump servicing, algae clearance and water plant management for healthy ponds.

✂️

Structural Pruning

Hard prune roses, wisteria and deciduous shrubs while dormant to promote vigorous regrowth.
