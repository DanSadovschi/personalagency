FAQ

Common questions.

01Are your prices fixed or can they change?

All quotes are fixed in writing before you book. The price you're given is the price you pay. No surprise charges on move day.

02Do you use your own crew?

Always. We never subcontract jobs. The same team who loads your belongings at your old address will unload them at the new one.

03What insurance do you carry?

All moves include goods in transit cover up to £50,000 as standard. Additional cover for high-value items is available — ask when booking.

04Do you offer a packing service?

Yes. Full or partial packing is available as an add-on. We supply all materials — boxes, tape, tissue paper and specialist containers.

05How far in advance should I book?

For weekends and month-end dates, 3–4 weeks is ideal. Weekday moves are often available with shorter notice. Contact us to check availability.

06What if completion is delayed?

Property chains slip — we know. Call us as early as possible and we'll do everything we can to accommodate the change without penalty.
