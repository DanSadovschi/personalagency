The Process

From Survey to Handover

No surprises. Every project follows the same clear five-step process so you always know what's happening and when.

01

Free Survey

We visit within 48 hours, assess the site, take measurements and understand your vision.

02

Fixed Quote

Detailed written quote within 5 days. Fixed price — no hidden costs, no surprise invoices.

03

Planning

We handle planning permission and building regulations submissions where required.

04

Build

Weekly progress updates, site foreman on-site daily. You're never in the dark.

05

Handover

Snagging list addressed, building regs sign-off, and your 10-year structural warranty issued.

What We Build

Our Specialisms

General builders with deep expertise across residential extensions, conversions and renovations throughout London.

01

Rear Extensions

Single and double-storey rear extensions. Bi-folds, skylights, structural steel — we design and build the lot.

02

Loft Conversions

Dormer, hip-to-gable, mansard. New bedroom, home office or en-suite — we handle planning and build.

03

Side Returns

Reclaim the dead space beside your kitchen. Side return extensions that transform ground floor living.

04

Garage Conversions

Permitted development, no planning needed in most cases. Habitable room ready in 4–6 weeks.

05

Full Renovations

Strip back to brick and start fresh. Full refurbs managed start to finish with a single fixed quote.

06

New Builds

Plot to completion. We work with architects and structural engineers to deliver standalone new-build homes.
