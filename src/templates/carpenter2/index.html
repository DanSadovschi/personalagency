How We Work

The Process

01
The Brief

We meet on-site or over video. You describe the space and your vision. We ask the right questions and listen carefully.

02
Design

Scaled drawings, mood boards and a fixed-price quote — delivered within one week. No surprises, no moving goalposts.

03
Materials

You visit the workshop to select your timber, finish and hardware in person. Touch and smell the wood before it becomes yours.

04
Build

Each piece is handbuilt by our craftsmen using traditional joinery techniques. No MDF. No shortcuts. No compromises.

05
Install

Our team installs, finishes and cleans up. You sign off on quality before we leave, backed by a five-year guarantee.
