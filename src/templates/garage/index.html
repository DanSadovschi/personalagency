What We Do

Our Services

From routine maintenance to complex engine repairs — our certified mechanics handle it all with precision and care.

Engine Diagnostics

Advanced OBD2 diagnostics to pinpoint faults fast. Full electronic scan with written report included.

MOT Testing

DVSA-authorised MOT centre. Pass first time or we'll fix the advisory items at a discounted rate.

Tyre Fitting

All major brands in stock. Same-day fitting, balancing, and TPMS reset. Budget to premium options.

Brake Service

Pads, discs, callipers and brake fluid. We use OEM-quality parts backed by a 2-year guarantee.

Oil & Fluids

Manufacturer-spec oil changes with genuine filters. Coolant, transmission, and power steering flush available.

Bodywork & Respray

Dent removal, scratch repair, and full or partial resprays. Colour-matched finish, lifetime warranty on paint.
